## Direction decision

Selected: the board is one material field with two interruptions — the river
changes its current, and the spine changes its state. Pieces sit in the field
rather than on a diagram.

Rejected: grok's two shore-colour climates. The traditional red/black face
pairs already carry side identity, and two large background climates would
replace the existing arcade field rather than synthesize with it.

## Capture bet

The victim face extinguishes instantly; its lacquer shell pinches inward for
one beat, then breaks into restrained dot debris. The field briefly reclaims
the intersection as one dim node before the capturing disc settles. No lasting
grave mark: a xiangqi piece is lifted away, not memorialized.

Failure condition: the event reads as a generic explosion. Success condition:
the order is legible — name out, shell closes, lacquer scatters, new piece sits.
