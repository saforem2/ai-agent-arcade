# Work card: VOID SEALS

## Fixed

- Standalone terminal mockup under `/tmp/xq-mock-codex/` only.
- Full xiangqi opening position.
- Fit a 113 x 53 terminal pane.
- Every piece fits a 14 x 14 braille-dot budget.
- No Chinese characters and no chess silhouettes.
- Seven mutually distinct piece identities, especially elephant versus cannon.

## Open

- Board density, colors, labels, negative-space grammar, and hierarchy.

## Source

- Real xiangqi's equal circular discs.
- Braille's strength as clustered mass.
- Xiangqi-native movement constraints: palace, diagonal guard, orthogonal chariot,
  blocked horse leg, cannon bore/screen, forward soldier.

## Directions considered

### A — Movement seals

Equal discs carry punched movement/role diagrams: palace, diagonal cut, paired
elephant eyes, rails, horse leg, cannon bore, forward wedge. Risk: a new symbol
language requires a one-line legend.

### B — Seven ritual objects

Equal discs carry pictograms of throne, shield, tusks, axle, hoof, muzzle, and
spearpoint. Risk: miniature illustration recreates the broken-line failure.

## Commit

Choose A. It keeps the marks geometric and void-heavy, and it makes the system
specifically xiangqi rather than a family of tiny rebadged chess silhouettes.
Relation: begin, from the brief's own material diagnosis.
